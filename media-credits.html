Illustrative model animation

The clay heads use the neutral template from FLAME 2023 Open, developed by the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, owned by the Max-Planck-Gesellschaft. Model reference: Tianye Li, Timo Bolkart, Michael J. Black, Hao Li, Javier Romero, Learning a model of facial shape and expression from 4D scans (2017).

The Open model is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0; see the Open Model License. Changes for these illustrations: hand-authored vertex movement, clay shading, displacement coloring, and animation. The clips are topic illustrations, not fitted people, experimental outputs, or evidence for the work under review.

Authored diagrams

The cinema animation redraws the high-level viewing, facial-sensing, and film-beat elements of the author's project overview. Its curve is authored for illustration. Abstract colored frames replace all film footage. It contains no participant observations or study screenshots.

The smart-glasses animation uses original unbranded glasses geometry, a drawn waveform, camera-frame icons, and a wireframe scene. It shows only the public audio-video capture topic, without device-specific methods or captured data.

All illustrations were made with conventional 3D rendering and deterministic drawing tools. No stock media or generative images or videos are used.
