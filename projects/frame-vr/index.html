Research project

FRAME VR

Facial-Reaction-Adaptive Movie Engine in VR

A standalone headset system that uses facial reactions to select film beats. FRAME VR includes BlendME II as its dataset and recognition component.

36
viewing-study participants
3
conditions: Static, Adaptive, Yoked

One project, from capture to cinema

FRAME VR includes the BlendME II dataset and recognition component: 45 corpus participants, 14 elicitation videos, and 5,602 annotated events, with participant-level benchmarking and stimulus-leakage controls. Facial reactions guide the selection of film beats in the standalone headset experience.

A separate 36-participant study compares Static, Adaptive, and Yoked viewing. This viewing study and the expression corpus are different samples.
